# Output templates

Every document starts with: title, captured date, sources used, what was not accessible, and the evidence labels in use. Keep tables tight; put detail behind them. Plain language, no em dashes.

## Campaign context (phase 1)

1. Goal in one sentence, and the definition of winning (Maps, organic, AI).
2. The client: table of field, value, source (name, market address, phone, hours, licenses, ratings, financing, guarantee, trust signals). Services relevant to the category. The differentiator.
3. Baseline pointers (filled after phase 3).
4. Known problems and risks, numbered.
5. Rules for collaborators: data honesty, client content rules, writing, outreach.
6. Tools and access: table of asset and status (GSC, GA4, market Google profile, CMS, GitHub, Tracker reports).
7. Open questions for the user and client.

## Prompt set (phase 2)

Table: #, status, intent, search query, AI prompt, why it earns a slot. Then "considered and cut" with reasons.

## Week 0 benchmark (phase 3)

1. Checklist: the seven questions with status (done, section, or not possible and why).
2. GA4: site-wide table (sessions, users, engaged sessions, key events; current, previous, change), monthly trend, market and category landing pages, limitations.
3. Search Console: site-wide table (clicks, impressions, CTR, position), monthly trend, branded demand, market pages (page level), market category queries (query level), what it means, data notes.
4. Visibility: headline KPI table with proposed Week 12 targets (AI mention rate, AI citation rate, core-prompt mentions, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Maps positions for head terms, organic top 10 count, Bing), scorecard by prompt and platform (M = mentioned, C = cited, number = position, NR, `.` not mentioned, `-` no result), mentions by platform, what it means (3 to 5 points), competitors, most-cited sites, caveats.

## Measurement audit (phase 4)

Summary, how it was checked (table of check, tool, what it proves), scorecard (9 items), detail per item, attribution findings, tag and account inventory, fix plan (proposed, nothing done), end-to-end test plan, open questions. Funnel test: summary, measurement, validation, reliability, form design, routing, what held up, priority order.

## Brand and entity audit (phase 5)

Executive summary; findings by severity (critical, high, medium, low), each with evidence and fix; source of truth pack (copy-paste name, address, phone, hours, categories, descriptions, `sameAs`); priority plan (top 10 in order, 7-day sprint, 30-day cleanup, monthly monitoring); NAP variations table and canonical version; collisions; category association; entity map with a loose-ends table (item, why it matters, who resolves, how).

## Site, page and backlink audits (phase 6)

Site: one-line read, what the numbers say, findings ranked for the goal, what to ignore, suggested order. Pages: table (page, target query, score, entity coverage, keyword in title/H1), patterns across pages, page-specific fixes, ignore. Backlinks: bottom line, profile at a glance (real linking sites, not raw totals), what the linking domains really are, where links land, technical link problems, lost links to reclaim, competitor comparison and gap, ranked actions with owner and status, KPIs to track weekly.

## Competitor gaps and opportunities (phase 7)

Gaps: the short version, gaps ranked by impact (each with evidence and the move), where the client already leads, fastest openings, decisions needed, caveats. Opportunities: directory and "best of" pages without the client (URL, type, cited by which platforms, competitors present), info pages, community threads, competitor profile pages, AI answers without the client, queries where the client isn't on page 1, Maps gaps, where the client is present, could not verify.

## Weekly check-in (phase 10)

Headline, KPI table (Week 0, last week, this week), wins, losses, shipped this week (from the log), blockers and handoffs with owners, next three actions.
